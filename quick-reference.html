1 · Quick-Reference Practice Sheet

All 24 movements of the Simplified Form, standing and seated cues side by side, condensed directly from the book’s instructions.

Check with your doctor before starting, especially with pre-existing conditions. Stop and rest if you feel pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.
#MovementStandingSeated
1Commencing FormRaise arms to shoulder height, then lower as knees settle.Arms float up to chest height, sink as you exhale.
2Part the Wild Horse's ManeStep out; front hand rises to eye height, back hand sinks.Extend leg forward; same-side hand rises, opposite hand sinks.
3White Crane Spreads Its WingsDraw foot in on toes; right hand arcs above forehead.Right hand rises above forehead, left hand settles near hip.
4Brush Knee and Twist StepStep forward; one hand brushes past knee, other pushes forward.Extend leg forward; same-side hand brushes knee, other pushes forward.
5Hand Strums the LuteWeight back, front heel down, hands meet like holding instrument.Both hands meet at chest, one higher, weight settles evenly.
6Step Back and Repulse MonkeyStep backward; one hand draws to ear, other pushes forward.Shift into chair back; draw one hand back, push other forward.
7Left Grasp the Bird's TailWard off, roll back, press, push, weight and torso turning.Same four arm actions; weight shifts gently between hips.
8Right Grasp the Bird's TailRepeat ward off, roll back, press, push, mirrored to the right.Same four connected actions, mirrored, weight shifting hip to hip.
9Single WhipWide stance, weight on one leg, other arm hooks out fully.Extend one arm into hook shape, weight settles on that hip.
10Wave Hands Like CloudsSide-step slowly, hands circle as weight rocks side to side.Both hands rise to chest height; torso turns slowly side to side.
11Single Whip (repeated)Repeat the wide hook-hand stance from movement nine.Repeat the seated hook-hand version from movement nine.
12High Pat on HorseWeight fully back; one hand draws to ear, one extends palm-up.One hand draws back near ear, other extends forward, palm up.
13Kick with Right HeelExtend leg slowly, heel leading; keep standing leg grounded.Extend one leg forward slowly, heel leading, toes lifted.
14Strike Ears with Both FistsFists arc inward and up beside head as weight shifts forward.Curl fists, arc them up beside head, weight settles forward slightly.
15Turn and Kick with Left HeelPivot to change direction, then mirror the heel kick left.Extend one leg forward slowly, heel leading, toes lifted.
16Left Lower Body and Stand on One Leg (Golden Rooster)Lower body, then stand on one leg; knee lifts to hip height.Lift knee toward chest, hold a breath or two, lower slowly.
17Right Lower Body and Stand on One Leg (Golden Rooster)Mirror to the other side; stand rooted, knee lifts to hip height.Repeat on the other side: lift knee, hold, lower with control.
18Fair Lady Works the ShuttlesTravel to a corner; one hand guards forehead, other pushes out.One hand guards forehead, other pushes out, torso turns to diagonal.
19Needle at Sea BottomFold forward from hips; one hand draws down toward floor.Fold forward gently, keeping the motion small and comfortable.
20Flash the ArmOne arm sweeps up to protect head, other pushes forward.Sweep one arm up and out, other pushes forward, torso opens.
21Turn, Deflect, Parry and PunchSmall defensive hand moves, then a slow, controlled forward punch.Same defensive moves and slow punch; torso turns instead of stepping.
22Apparent Close UpHands draw to chest, then push forward as weight shifts forward.Draw hands to chest, push forward, weight settles back then forward.
23Cross HandsFeet return to shoulder width; hands cross in front of chest.Feet flat and even; hands cross in front of chest.
24Closing FormHands separate, lower slowly to sides as weight settles evenly.Hands float up, then sink slowly to rest on thighs.
