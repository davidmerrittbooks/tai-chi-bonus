6 · Printable Wall Poster (All 24 Movements)

Print this page and pin it near where you practice. It is not meant to be read like the practice sheet — it is meant for a quick glance mid-flow, to jog your memory of what comes next without breaking your pace.

#MovementStandingSeated
1Commencing FormArms rise to shoulder height, then sink.Arms float to chest height, then sink.
2Part the Wild Horse’s ManeStep out; front hand rises, back hand sinks.Extend leg; same-side hand rises, other sinks.
3White Crane Spreads Its WingsDraw foot in; right hand arcs overhead.Right hand rises overhead, left settles at hip.
4Brush Knee and Twist StepStep forward; hand brushes knee, other pushes.Extend leg; hand brushes knee, other pushes.
5Hand Strums the LuteWeight back, heel down, hands meet.Hands meet at chest, weight settles evenly.
6Step Back and Repulse MonkeyStep back; one hand draws to ear.Shift into chair back; draw hand to ear.
7Left Grasp the Bird’s TailWard off, roll back, press, push.Same four actions; weight shifts hip to hip.
8Right Grasp the Bird’s TailRepeat, mirrored to the right.Same actions, mirrored, weight shifting hip to hip.
9Single WhipWide stance; one arm hooks out fully.Extend arm into hook; weight settles on hip.
10Wave Hands Like CloudsSide-step slowly; hands circle with weight.Hands rise to chest; torso turns side to side.
11Single Whip (repeated)Repeat the wide hook-hand stance.Repeat the seated hook-hand version.
12High Pat on HorseWeight back; one hand draws to ear.One hand draws back, other extends palm-up.
13Kick with Right HeelExtend leg slowly, heel leading.Extend leg forward, heel leading, toes lifted.
14Strike Ears with Both FistsFists arc up beside head.Curl fists, arc up beside head.
15Turn and Kick with Left HeelPivot, then mirror the heel kick left.Extend leg forward, heel leading, toes lifted.
16Golden Rooster (Left)Lower body, then stand on one leg.Lift knee toward chest, hold, lower slowly.
17Golden Rooster (Right)Mirror to the other side.Repeat on the other side, lower with control.
18Fair Lady Works the ShuttlesTravel to a corner; hand guards forehead.Hand guards forehead, other pushes out.
19Needle at Sea BottomFold forward from hips; hand draws down.Fold forward gently, keeping motion small.
20Flash the ArmOne arm sweeps up to protect head.Sweep arm up and out, other pushes forward.
21Turn, Deflect, Parry and PunchSmall defensive moves, then a slow punch.Same moves; torso turns instead of stepping.
22Apparent Close UpHands draw to chest, then push forward.Draw hands to chest, push forward.
23Cross HandsFeet return to shoulder width; hands cross.Feet flat and even; hands cross at chest.
24Closing FormHands separate, lower slowly to sides.Hands float up, then sink to rest on thighs.
