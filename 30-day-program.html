3 · Your 30-Day Practice Program

A starting template, not a prescription — the same honest approach as the two-week calendar in Chapter 7, stretched out further. Foundation work in week one, the first half of the Simplified 24-Form in weeks two and three, the second half in week four, and a review in the final days. Two days a week are deliberately not tai chi days. Swap in whichever postures you've actually learned so far — the point is the rhythm, not keeping exact pace with the calendar.

If you practice on twenty to twenty-two days out of these thirty, the program has done its job — the same honest math the rest of this book uses. A short session repeated often adds up to more than a perfect streak chased and then abandoned.
