4 · Printable 30-Day Habit Tracker

A simple paper tracker, the same idea described in Chapter 8: a date, a checkmark, and one honest word about how the session felt — steady, rushed, off-balance, calm. Print it and keep it somewhere you'll actually see it: the refrigerator, next to the coffee maker, on a nightstand. A week of checkmarks is modest evidence, but it's evidence you produced yourself.
